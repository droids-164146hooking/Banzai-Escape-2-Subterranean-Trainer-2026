## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Banzai Escape 2: Subterranean**. This trainer enhances missions in Banzai Escape 2: Subterranean by providing control over player survivability, ammunition, reload behavior, resources, loot, and progression-related systems. It is designed around the game's third-person shooting, randomized weapons, nano-plugin powerups, procedural mission routes, and upgradeable lobby.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed solo experience, faster build testing, easier experimentation with randomized weapons and nano-plugin powerups, or assistance while replaying procedural missions.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Move through the underground networks in third-person view, follow mission objectives, and use the available routes through procedurally assembled map segments. The trainer does not replace movement controls; activate gameplay options after the mission has loaded.

### Tools & Inventory

Manage randomized firearms, powerups, loot, and character equipment between missions. Weapon and nano-plugin drops can have different stats, so use the trainer to test builds, preserve resources, or experiment with newly acquired gear.

### World & Environment

Missions take place in underground dungeon networks with changing routes and objectives. Explore each area, collect rewards, complete the assigned mission, and return to the hub to use progression and upgrade systems. Solo and two-player cooperative modes are supported by the game.

### Combat & Weapons

Fight aggressive enemies from factions occupying the underground facilities, including Lanun and SPECTRE forces. Use the trainer's health, ammunition, reload, and skill options to experiment with different weapons and powerup combinations during intense firefights.
